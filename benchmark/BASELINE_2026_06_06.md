## Performance Analysis

### Fast Instances (< 0.01s)
- **Algebra/XOR**: Preprocessing eliminates most variables via resolution
- **Arg chain**: Efficient backjumping + phase saving
- **Small PHP (SAT)**: Few conflicts, good variable selection

### Slow Instances (sudoku: 9.4s)
- **Root cause**: Linear scanning of 8,586 clauses per propagation step
- **Impact**: ~1000x slower than MiniSat (which uses watched literals)
- **Characteristics**: 
  - 729 variables, 8,586 clauses after preprocessing
  - All clauses are ternary (3 literals) - handled by watched literals
  - 10,002 conflicts, 14,632 decisions
  - Average learned clause LBD: 8
